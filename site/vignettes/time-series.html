Fit AR, MA, and ARMA

Conditional Gaussian fits
ArimaResult ar1 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ar(1));
ArimaResult ar3 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ar(3));
ArimaResult ma1 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ma(1));
ArimaResult arma = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.arma(2, 1));

if (!arma.converged())
    throw new IllegalStateException(
        arma.convergenceMessage());

MA signs match R. Parameters are transformed to preserve stationarity/invertibility. Compare AICc only among models fitted to the same response definition.

Exact diffuse and missing-series likelihood

DiffuseArima uses symbolic diffuse Kalman recursions for integrated and seasonal models with missing observations. SparseMissingSeries filters with state-sized covariance instead of a dense observation covariance. Missing observations advance uncertainty without a measurement update; forecasts propagate the resulting filtered state.

Smooth long historical series

Historical states and signals
var regression = ArimaRegression.fit(
    responseWithNaN, design, order, options);
ArimaSmoothing.Result history = regression.smoothErrors();

double smoothedSignal = history.signal()[missingDate];
double signalVariance = history.signalVariance()[missingDate];

ArimaSmoothing estimates symbolic diffuse levels through the finite-state innovations and then runs an exact backward information smoother. It conditions each historical state on all observed dates, including observations after a gap. Work is linear in the number of dates and stores state-sized matrices per returned date; no observation-sized covariance matrix or date cap remains. The state order is still limited to 128, and the requested covariance output necessarily occupies O(n r²) memory. Historical moments condition on fitted regression coefficients, dynamics, and innovation variance; they do not include parameter-estimation uncertainty.

Include fitted-parameter uncertainty in forecasts

Conditional and parameter-aware intervals
ArimaForecast conditional =
    regression.forecastConditional(futureDesign, .95);
if (regression.jointParameterInferenceAvailable()) {
    ArimaForecast full =
        regression.forecastWithParameterUncertainty(
            futureDesign, .95);
    double[] estimates = regression.jointParameterEstimates();
    double[] joint = regression.jointParameterCovariance();
}

forecastConditional reports process uncertainty conditional on the fitted regression and dynamics; forecast remains its compatibility alias. The parameter-aware method adds delta-method variance from the joint regression/dynamic covariance, retaining their cross-covariances. Its observed-information Hessian is computed and cached only when joint inference or a parameter-aware forecast is requested; ordinary fits, smoothing, and conditional forecasts remain on the profiled path. The matching estimate and covariance order is regression coefficients, AR, MA, seasonal AR, then seasonal MA, keeping seasonal coordinates separate from multiplicative effective polynomials. Innovation-variance estimation uncertainty is excluded, and unresolved joint information fails explicitly instead of silently reverting to a conditional interval.

Thirteen R fixtures cover coefficients, likelihood, forecast means, and forecast SEs. A 50,000-position missing-series regression test runs under a bounded heap, and a 5,000-date smoothing regression exercises the formerly capped path. R uses finite diffuse initialization, so its small likelihood difference is documented rather than hidden. Diffuse coefficient covariance and SEs include seasonal terms and joint drift/dynamic uncertainty, with unresolved information suppressed. Regression and smoothing contracts.
