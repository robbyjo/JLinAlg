Conditional models for integration and forecast, an exact stationary Gaussian path, diagnostic utilities, small-order search, and profiled ARIMA-error mixed models.

Fit AR, MA, and ARMA

Conditional Gaussian fits
ArimaResult ar1 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ar(1));
ArimaResult ar3 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ar(3));
ArimaResult ma1 = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.ma(1));
ArimaResult arma = Arima.fit(
    series, ArimaOrder.arma(2, 1));

if (!arma.converged())
    throw new IllegalStateException(
        arma.convergenceMessage());

MA signs match R. Parameters are transformed to preserve stationarity/invertibility. Compare AICc only among models fitted to the same response definition.

Difference and model seasonality

Monthly SARIMA and forecast
ArimaOptions options = ArimaOptions.builder()
    .seasonalOrder(
        SeasonalArimaOrder.of(1, 1, 1, 12))
    .build();

ArimaResult sarima = Arima.fit(
    monthly, ArimaOrder.arima(1, 1, 1),
    options);
ArimaForecast forecast = sarima.forecast(12, .95);

includeMean belongs to undifferenced models. With exactly one total difference, use includeDrift(true) to model deterministic drift.

Check innovations

ACF, PACF, Ljung–Box
double[] acf = arma.residualAutocorrelation(20);
double[] pacf =
    TimeSeriesDiagnostics.partialAutocorrelation(
        arma.innovations(), 20);
LjungBoxResult lb = arma.ljungBox(20);

Choose diagnostic lags before inspecting results and supplement formal tests with residual and intervention plots.

Use exact stationary likelihood

Missing observations and panels
ExactArmaResult exact = ExactArma.fit(
    seriesWithNaN, ArimaOrder.arma(1, 1),
    true, BackendPolicy.PREFERRED);

ExactArmaResult panel = ExactArma.fitPanel(
    List.of(siteOne, siteTwo, siteThree),
    ArimaOrder.ar(1), true,
    BackendPolicy.PREFERRED);

Missing stationary observations are marginalized through the Toeplitz covariance. Integrated models are intentionally rejected by this exact path.

Search small orders

Exhaustive AICc selection
ArimaSelectionResult selected =
    AutomaticArima.select(series, 3, 1, 3);
ArimaResult best = selected.bestModel();

Review every candidate's convergence and diagnostics rather than treating AICc selection as proof of the data-generating order.

Profile ARIMA errors in an LMM

Correlated residual mixed model
ArimaErrorLmmResult fit =
    ArimaErrorLinearMixedModel.fit(
        response, fixedDesign, randomTerms,
        ArimaOrder.arma(1, 1),
        ArimaErrorLmmOptions.builder()
            .remlOptions(RemlOptions.defaults())
            .build(),
        BackendPolicy.PREFERRED);

Differenced fits difference response and every fixed/random design column together. A level intercept becomes zero; represent drift with a level-scale time trend.
