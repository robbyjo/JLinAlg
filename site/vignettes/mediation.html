Mediation without sampling.

Three Gaussian models estimate the treatment-to-mediator, mediator-to-outcome, direct, and total paths.

Fit the component models

double[] treatment = {-2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3};
double[] mediator = {0.2, 1.1, 2.0, 3.2, 3.9, 5.1};
double[] outcome = {1.1, 1.9, 3.3, 4.8, 6.0, 7.7};
MediationResult result = MediationAnalysis.fit(outcome, treatment, mediator);
double indirect = result.indirectEffect().estimate();

The optional covariate matrix must not contain an intercept: the API supplies one. Defaults reject missing observations. Selecting MissingDataPolicy.OMIT uses a common complete-row sample across mediator, outcome, and total-effect models.

Interpret the indirect effect

The indirect estimate is a × b. Its first-order Sobel SE is sqrt(b² Var(a) + a² Var(b)), with normal inference and no bootstrap, Monte Carlo, or posterior draws. This omits cross-model covariance under the documented assumptions; it is not a general correlated-error or robust mediation estimator. Gaussian linear modeling alone does not establish causal identification.

Mixed effects and pedigrees

RandomEffectTerm subject = RandomEffectTerm.randomIntercept("subject", subjectIds);
MediationMixedResult mixed = MediationAnalysis.fitMixed(
    outcome, treatment, mediator, covariates, List.of(subject),
    RemlOptions.defaults(), 0.95, BackendPolicy.CPU);

fitPedigree accepts sparse numerator-relationship precision terms and optional ordinary terms. The same random structure is fitted separately in all three component models. Check each component's convergence and the assumptions behind cross-model independence before inference.
