Model proportions and precision.

Classical beta maximum likelihood for responses strictly inside (0,1), with separate mean and precision predictors.

CLI-only beta regression

java -jar build/cli/jlinalg-0.3.3.jar beta-regression \
  --input proportions.tsv --response proportion --mean dose \
  --precision batch_score --out beta-coefficients.tsv

The example uses shell continuation syntax; on PowerShell replace each trailing backslash with a backtick, or use one line. Numeric CSV/TSV input supplies proportion, dose, and batch_score columns.

Understand the parameterization

Mean μ and precision φ give beta shape parameters μφ and (1−μ)φ. The specialized fixed-effect fitter uses analytic scores and expected-information covariance. Zero/one values are outside the classical beta support; do not silently clip them into the interval.

Grouped and pedigree effects

BetaMixedModel uses sparse Gaussian random effects in the logit mean with constant precision, including pedigree A⁻¹. It uses first-order Laplace inference, not the scalar binomial/Poisson adaptive-quadrature API. Precision-side random effects and extended-support beta mixtures are not part of this path.
