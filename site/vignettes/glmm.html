Generalized mixed models—with the approximation named.

Use PQL or first-order Laplace likelihoods for binomial, count, zero-inflated, and pedigree models while keeping each estimator's limits explicit.

Construct random covariance

PQL uses the same observation-scale random covariance bases as dense REML. Build a group block without hand-writing a matrix:

Random intercept basis
VarianceComponent group =
    VarianceComponent.randomIntercept(
        "clinic", clinicIds);

Fit a count mixed model

Poisson PQL
GlmmPqlResult fit = GlmmPql.fit(
    counts, fixedDesign,
    GlmFamilies.poisson(),
    List.of(group));

if (!fit.converged())
    throw new IllegalStateException(
        fit.convergenceMessage());

AssociationStatistics inference =
    fit.associationStatistics();

The full overload accepts positive prior weights, log exposure as an offset, PQL iteration controls, and a backend policy.

Fit a binary pedigree GLMM

Pedigree binomial PQL
PedigreeGlmmPqlResult pedigreeFit =
    PedigreeGlmmPql.fit(
        affected, fixedDesign,
        GlmFamilies.binomial(),
        observationAnimalIds, pedigree);

Supported families include binomial, Poisson, fixed-size negative-binomial, and appropriate quasi families. Gaussian responses belong in exact REML/LMM APIs.

Fit a frequentist zero-inflated pedigree model

NB2-ZINB sparse Laplace
PedigreeRandomEffectTerm additive =
    PedigreeRandomEffectTerm.of(
        "individual", observationIndividualIds,
        pedigree);

ZeroInflatedMixedResult fit =
    SparseZeroInflatedMixedModel
        .fitNegativeBinomial(
            counts,
            countDesign, countColumns,
            zeroDesign, zeroColumns,
            sizeDesign, sizeColumns,
            List.of(additive.randomEffect()),
            List.of(additive.precision()),
            logExposure,
            ZeroInflatedMixedOptions.defaults(),
            BackendPolicy.PREFERRED);

BOBYQA optimizes fixed, NB2-size, and log-variance parameters. Sparse damped Newton iterations find the count-side random-effect mode, and the same observed Hessian supplies the Laplace determinant. The result distinguishes the conditional count mean, structural-zero probability, unconditional mean, and total zero mass. Zero-process random effects and correlated two-process pedigree covariance remain explicitly tracked work.

Interpret the working fit

Each outer iteration creates a GLM working response and known diagonal residual covariance, estimates random covariance scales by REML, then updates the random linear predictor.

PQL is not a marginal GLMM likelihoodIt can be biased for rare binary events, small clusters, and large random-effect variance. Satterthwaite or Kenward–Roger options apply to the final working linearization—not the exact non-Gaussian model.

Report the estimator as PQL, check both inner and outer convergence, and avoid likelihood comparisons with future Laplace/AGQ fits.
