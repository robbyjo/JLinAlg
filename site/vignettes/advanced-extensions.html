Closing the advanced analysis boundaries.

Seven focused extensions add missing-data SEM, correlated meta-regression, sparse unstructured mixed effects, diffuse and missing time series, generalized MR, quadrature GLMM, and selection-aware penalized inference.

Bounded contractsEach extension is deterministic and tested. The APIs document where they are a practical reference path rather than a claim of complete lavaan, metafor, lme4, forecast, TwoSampleMR, adaptive-quadrature, or polyhedral-inference parity.

SEM and meta-analysis

SemFiml estimates Gaussian missing-pattern moments with EM; LatentMeasurement provides principal-factor measurement; SemInference provides ordinal thresholds, sandwich corrections, modification indices, and indirect-effect delta inference. MetaMultilevelRegression fits GLS moderators against a full study covariance matrix, while MetaBiasCorrections provides PET, PEESE, and labelled median-reflection trim-and-fill.

Mixed models and time series

SparseUnstructuredCorrelatedModel estimates a lower-Cholesky random covariance shape with sparse refits and is reachable from CompiledMixedFormula.fitSparseUnstructured. DiffuseArima reports integrated-model diffuse-state accounting, and SparseMissingSeries uses the exact observed-pattern stationary ARMA covariance path.

MR and GLMM

mr-estimate --method ivw-generalized-random --ld ld.tsv --plot mr.svg

The estimator CLI now accepts LD matrices for generalized IVW/Egger and the conditional alias, plus per-instrument sampling covariance for overlap-aware IVW. GlmmQuadrature supplies a fixed 10-node Gaussian quadrature alternative for scalar random-intercept binomial/Poisson models.

Penalized selection

SelectionAwarePenalizedInference selects the active set on one deterministic data split and fits inference on held-out rows, preserving the separation needed for honest post-selection tests.

Verification

Run ./gradlew check. Focused tests cover each extension, including MR CLI parsing, sparse unstructured refits, FIML missing patterns, quadrature fitting, and held-out penalized inference.
