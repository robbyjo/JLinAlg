Advanced statistical extensions

Jointly fitted models, explicit assumptions, and independent R validation.

September 8 audit repairsThese implementations replace earlier approximations and incomplete paths. The validation report records accuracy, measured speed, reproduction commands, and remaining limits.

SEM

Sem.fit jointly estimates latent RAM paths and structural means. SemFiml maximizes the constrained observed-pattern likelihood directly. SemOrdinal jointly fits thresholds and latent structure with probit pairwise ML; fitPairwiseMissing adds available-pair missing ordinal PML under MCAR. Full covariance, robust/cluster inference, continuous-model modification indices, and indirect delta inference are available. Separate joint mixed MAR likelihood, DWLS/WLSMV and multigroup APIs now complement PML, together with ordinal likelihood modification indices and FIML scaling. SEM vignette →

Meta-analysis

Joint ML/REML random-moderator covariance, correlated CR0/CR1/CR2 inference, PET/PEESE, and actual L0/R0/Q0 trim-and-fill are checked against metafor and clubSandwich. Covariance computation is dense, and publication diagnostics do not establish or eliminate bias. Meta-analysis →

Sparse mixed models

Multiple estimated unstructured covariance blocks, model-derived Satterthwaite/KR inference, formula weights and offsets, and nuisance-optimized ML profile refits use sparse equations. A 12,000-row, 3,000-random-coefficient test runs with a 96 MB heap. R is faster on some fits; boundary inference and combined unstructured/pedigree optimization remain limited. Mixed models →

Integrated and missing time series

Exact diffuse Kalman recursions replace differenced bookkeeping. Missing-series likelihood and forecasts use state-sized storage, including seasonal and integrated models. R comparisons document its finite-diffuse initialization difference; diffuse coefficient covariance now includes joint drift uncertainty. ARIMA regression and bounded dense historical smoothing are available through separate APIs. Time series →

MR workflow

mr-estimate --input harmonized.tsv --method conditional --joint --ld ld.tsv

Generalized LD-aware and overlap-aware estimators, native SVG plots, conditional instrument tests, and conditional forward/backward signal selection are available. conditional requires --joint or --condition-on. Instrument association p-values are distinct from causal-estimate p-values. Signed allele-aligned LD and Gaussian summary-score assumptions are required. MR →

Adaptive GLMM integration

Mode/curvature-adaptive Gauss–Hermite integration with node refinement supports independent scalar Gaussian random intercepts for binomial and Poisson data, trials and offsets. Covariance inference and zero-variance checks are included. This is not multidimensional or pedigree AGQ. GLMM →

Selection-aware and other regressions

Weighted held-out inference and fixed-penalty Gaussian polyhedral LASSO/elastic-net inference are separate APIs. The latter requires independently known noise SD and fixed tuning choices. Multivariate OLS, multinomial, exact and smoothed quantile, kernel, R-compatible supersmoother, and Robinson HC3 regressions have independent fixtures. QuantileRegressionInference adds exact-fit covariance from supplied conditional densities or an iid residual kernel with caller-set bandwidth; automatic iid density bandwidths, mixed product-kernel undersmoothing, cluster/HAC quantile covariance and mass-point marginal intervals now have separate APIs and assumptions.
