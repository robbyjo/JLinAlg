Separate shared signals from linked signals.

Align regional variants, colocalize every retained SuSiE signal pair, and carry H0-H4 plus the H4-conditional variant posterior into MR interpretation.

CLI-only workflowThe colocalization tutorial starts with two SuSiE effect tables and follows them through H0-H4 and conditional shared-variant outputs. This CLI command requires current jlinalg-0.3.5-SNAPSHOT.jar or a later release; it is not in v0.3.4.

Align before fitting

Use the same genome build, allele convention, and ancestry-matched LD for both traits. Remove duplicate or ambiguous identifiers before fine mapping. JLinAlg intersects variant IDs in trait-1 order and reports the retained common set.

ColocSusieResult coloc = ColocSusie.analyze(
    exposureFineMap, outcomeFineMap, ColocOptions.defaults());

Priors and overlap

Defaults match coloc::coloc.susie: p1 and p2 are 1e-4, p12 is 5e-6, and signal pairs with less than 0.5 posterior overlap are trimmed. Inspect commonVariants() and skippedSignalPairs(), and repeat the analysis under defensible prior choices.

Read H0-H4

H0 means neither trait is associated; H1/H2 mean only one is associated; H3 favors distinct causal variants; H4 favors one shared variant. sharedVariantPosterior(pair) is conditional on H4, so a sharp variant posterior cannot rescue weak H4 evidence.

MR and xWAS hand-off

For each retained cis-MR hit, fine-map exposure and outcome separately, then report the MR estimate with H3, H4, overlap diagnostics, priors, and leading shared variants. Strong MR with H3 favored over H4 warns that LD between distinct signals may explain the result.

ValidationThe implementation is tested against coloc 5.2.3 for single- and multi-signal fixtures. Colocalization supports locus interpretation; it does not itself establish causality.
